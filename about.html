Who are we?

Two Generations, One Community

Born from a beat-up food truck with a single grill, Midas Burger has grown into three locations in the Jacksonville Metro area, thanks to one simple formula: exceptional food, an energetic atmosphere, and a deep commitment to our community. Now a second-generation family-run local favorite, we deliver the same quality and consistency that made us a Jacksonville staple from day one.

Beyond the kitchen, our family partners with local schools, churches, and volunteers to host seasonal food drives and support Jacksonville beach cleanups. Serving great burgers is only part of our mission; it is also serving our community!
